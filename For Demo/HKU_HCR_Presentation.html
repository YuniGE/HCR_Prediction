1. Conservative Threshold

Target: Current HCPs
Formula: Predicted × 1.07
Goal: Defense & Monitoring

Exceptional: >200% of threshold.
Solid: Safely above conservative line (~90% confidence).
Marginal: Close to the line. Risk zone.

2. Standard Threshold

Target: Non-Current HCPs
Formula: Predicted x 1.03
Goal: Offense & Growth

High Potential: Very close to becoming HCP.
Mid Potential: Within striking distance.
Low Potential: Long-term tracking.

Risk Monitoring (Marginal)
